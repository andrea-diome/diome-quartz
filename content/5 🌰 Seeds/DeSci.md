DeSci, short for Decentralized Science, is an emerging field at the intersection of blockchain technology and scientific research. It aims to leverage decentralized technologies, particularly those underpinning cryptocurrencies and smart contracts, to address various systemic issues within the traditional scientific research ecosystem. DeSci is seen as a part of the broader movement towards open science but with a specific focus on utilizing blockchain and Web3 technologies to foster transparency, accessibility, and collaboration in scientific endeavors.

DeSci addresses several critical problems in the traditional scientific research landscape:
- **Access and Openness**: Many scientific research outputs are locked behind paywalls, limiting access to those affiliated with institutions that can afford expensive subscriptions. DeSci projects use blockchain to host and share scientific papers and data, ensuring they are accessible to anyone with internet access, free of charge or at a much lower cost than traditional journal subscriptions.
- **Funding and Resource Allocation**: The current funding system is often criticized for being overly bureaucratic, opaque, and biased, potentially stifling innovation and slowing down research progress. Utilizing cryptocurrencies and smart contracts, DeSci aims to facilitate direct, transparent, and efficient funding of research projects, potentially bypassing traditional funding bodies and reducing administrative overhead.
- **Data Integrity and Reproducibility**: Issues with data manipulation, fraud, and a lack of reproducibility of research findings are significant concerns in the scientific community. Solutions employ blockchain's immutable ledger to securely store research data, making it tamper-proof and verifiable, which can help in addressing the reproducibility crisis.
- **Recognition and Reward Systems**: The traditional "publish or perish" model emphasizes high-impact publications as the primary metric for success, which can devalue other forms of scientific contributions. DeSci projects are developing new models for recognizing and rewarding all forms of scientific contributions, not just publication in high-impact journals, through mechanisms like tokenomics that can offer financial or reputational incentives.
