## What
[Sensemaking Networks](https://paragraph.xyz/@sense-nets/sense-nets-intro) is a groundbreaking project aimed at revolutionizing scientific communication by integrating AI-augmented social media networks with decentralized semantic nanopublishing. This initiative facilitates enhanced information flow and bolsters participants’ agency and data sovereignty, making scientific discourse more accessible, inclusive, and actionable.
## Who
Developed by Ronen Tamari, Entrepreneur-In-Residence (EIR) at Astera Institute.
## Where & When
The project is currently in the development stage.
## Why
The initiative seeks to address the fragmented and siloed nature of scientific communication, leveraging the sensemaking layer of science that includes non-traditional publications like annotations, reviews, and social media commentary. It aims to democratize scientific discourse by making it more inclusive, accessible, and integrated, thereby addressing the limitations of existing science publishing infrastructure.
## How
### Key Principles
- Enhances interoperability and information flow through semantic nanopublishing.
- Empowers participants with greater agency over their data.
- Improves discoverability of research and encourages broader adoption by making semantic publishing as easy as posting on social media.
- Facilitates collaborative communication, contextualization, curation, and assessment of research.
- Promotes inclusivity by bridging the gap between scientists and citizen scientists.
- Addresses the challenges of data siloing and enclosure on commercial platforms.
- Aims to recover semantics in social media publishing for more accessible and open science discourse.
### Functions
1. **Recovering Existing Nanopublications from Social Media**: This effort focuses on using Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools to identify and recover valuable scientific knowledge shared on social media. The goal is to parse social media posts into nanopublication templates, enriching the scientific record with structured, machine-actionable data that's more useful than raw text. 
2. **Building a Decentralized Semantic Nanopublishing Social App**: This initiative aims to create a federated/decentralized social network app where users can engage in semantic nanopublishing. The app will support diverse publishing formats, enhance the feedback loop with rapid, transparent publications, and prevent data siloing.
