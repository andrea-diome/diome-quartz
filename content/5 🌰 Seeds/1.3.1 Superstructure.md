## Overview
### What is it?
Superstructure is the realm of beliefs, values, and worldviews in a society, shaping and being shaped by how we perceive and interact with the world.
### Why is it important?
> *The most important domination was the mental universe of the colonized, the control, through culture, of how people perceived themselves and their relationship to the world. Economic and political control can never be complete or effective without mental control. To control a people’s culture is to control their tools of self-definition in relationship to others.* [Thiongo, Decolonising the Mind](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/986506/7/Indigenous_Protocol_and_AI_2020.pdf)

- All humans are "programmed" from birth, absorbing the narratives and belief systems of their environment. This programming shapes how we see ourselves and our relationship with the world.
- Our beliefs shape how we collectively experience reality; we tend to see what we expect to see.
- The superstructure both shapes and is shaped by our tools, technologies, and social structures.
- As technology advances, it profoundly influences our collective programming. For example, social media has revolutionized our perceptions of community, identity, and even truth itself, transforming how we conceptualize reality and our place within it.
### How does it need to change?
- Current superstructure is shaped by narratives of domination, exploitation, and a perceived separateness from nature. 
- Many of our decisions, including technological development, are driven primarily by the pursuit of profit and economic growth.
- Therefore, our technologies prioritize turning us into consumers and our planet into a money-making machine.
- Science, technology, money or markets alone lack an inherent value system to guide their application. 
- We need to transition to a superstructure that recognizes that we are nature and depend on our relationships to the rest of the planetary ecosystem, and each other.
- We need to rediscover and cultivate a shared set of ethical principles and value systems that can steer technological progress towards regenerative outcomes, without resorting to dogmatism or rejecting scientific knowledge.
- We must reimagine our educational and cultural systems to communicate and embody this shift.
