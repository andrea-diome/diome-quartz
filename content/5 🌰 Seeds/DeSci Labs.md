## What
[DeSci Labs](https://desci.com/) is an initiative to revolutionize how scientific research is published, accessed, and credited. It aims to replace static PDFs with dynamic, interoperable research ecosystems, enhancing trust through verified badges and ensuring transparent credit tracking. It leverages decentralized web technologies to empower researchers with seamless access and control over their work.
## Who
Designed for researchers, publishers, and institutions.
## Where & When
Accessible online.
## Why
The motivation behind DeSci Labs is to address the limitations of traditional scientific publishing by creating an open, interoperable, and collaborative platform. It aims to overcome issues such as dead links, content drift, data silos, and the restricted expressivity of scientific publications. By doing so, DeSci Labs seeks to enhance the reproducibility, accessibility, and integrity of scientific research.
## How
### Key principles
- **Openness and Accessibility**: The infrastructure is designed to be open and accessible to everyone, with no single entity in control.
- **Interoperability**: Publications are reimagined as rich, interconnected research ecosystems, promoting seamless collaboration.
- **Trust and Quality**: Enhanced trust in scientific findings through digitally signed attestations, rewarding high-quality research.
- **Transparent Credit**: Credit for contributions is transparently tracked, with provisions for recognizing substantial contributions from new collaborators.
- **Persistent Access**: Ensures durable access to work through simple, usable persistent identifiers, eliminating common issues like dead links and content drift.
### Functions
- **DeSci Nodes**: Central hubs for manuscripts, data, and code, all stored in one place, facilitating a more interconnected and richly expressive form of scientific publishing.
- **Decentralized Publishing**: Utilizes a decentralized web protocol to ensure data sovereignty, integrity, and accessibility.
- **Enhanced Collaboration**: A platform for scientists to earn through innovative representations of their work, and for institutions to manage research data effectively.
- **Semantic Nanopublishing**: Incorporates semantic nanopublishing to enhance the interoperability and flow of information, making research easier to reproduce, share, and credit.
- **Sustainable and Inclusive Funding**: Introduces the DeSci Fund for Scientific Innovation, enabling scientists to earn money for their research, thereby supporting the financial sustainability of open science practices.
