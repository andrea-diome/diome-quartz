## What
Leveraging blockchain technology, [Freeos](https://www.freeos.io/) allows individuals to earn FREEOS tokens through collective decision-making and voting on monetary policies that affect the token's health and value.
### Where & When
Pilot happening now in New Zealand
### Why
The purpose of Freeos is to democratize income, offering a system where cooperation and community decision-making lead to personal and collective benefits. 
### How
#### Key Principles
- **Democratic Participation**: Each user has an equal vote in the system's monetary policy.
- **Cooperation**: Freeos emphasizes communal values, where decisions are made for the collective good.
- **Community Empowerment**: Users are empowered to have a direct influence on their economic circumstances.
#### Functions
- **Weekly Income**: Users receive FREEOS tokens as income for their participation.
- **Voting System**: A simple, democratic process where users vote on policies affecting the token's value. By voting on monetary policies, users support the token's stability and ensure the system's longevity, aligning individual rewards with the community's welfare.
- **Identity Verification**: To ensure one person equals one vote, identity verification is done through protonkyc.com.
