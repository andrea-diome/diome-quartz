FAIR data principles are guidelines that aim to enhance the utility and accessibility of data in the digital age. These principles emphasize the importance of making data Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable. The FAIR data principles were first proposed in a [2016 publication by Wilkinson et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618), and have since become a cornerstone for researchers, librarians, funders, and policymakers who are working towards improving the infrastructure supporting the reuse of scholarly data.
