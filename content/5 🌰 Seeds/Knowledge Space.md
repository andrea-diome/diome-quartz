[Knowledge Space](https://gist.github.com/tkuhn/a3842683b810f1e0dbc72a021083ea31) aims to overcome the limitations of current web technologies and knowledge management systems by establishing a more structured, interconnected, and trustworthy digital knowledge ecosystem. By being open and decentralized, it aims to be a collaborative space where knowledge is continually updated, corrected, and enriched by contributions from across the globe.

Some specific aspects and innovations of the Knowledge Space are:
• **Knowledge Records:** Small, atomic pieces of information are encapsulated in knowledge records, each tagged with metadata (like creation date, authorship) and a unique, cryptographically generated identifier. This structure ensures that each piece of knowledge is traceable, verifiable, and reusable.
• **Dynamic Knowledge Management:** Knowledge records can supersede or retract previous records, allowing the knowledge base to evolve over time. Collections of knowledge records can be linked, enabling complex structures of information that reflect the latest understanding and consensus.
• **Universal and Extensible Vocabulary:** The system uses a universal vocabulary expressed in formal logic, making it easier for both humans and computers to understand and process information. This approach facilitates interoperability and integration across different domains and applications.
• **Publishing/Lookup and Query Services:** The ecosystem includes various services that enable knowledge agents to publish new knowledge records, look up existing ones, and perform complex queries. These services are designed to be redundant and independent, ensuring robustness and reliability.
• **Trust Mechanisms:** It integrates trust through redundancy (multiple copies of data to ensure availability and integrity) and cryptographic techniques (for secure, tamper-evident knowledge records). The ecosystem allows for the creation of knowledge settings that define trusted sources and algorithms for establishing trustworthiness.
