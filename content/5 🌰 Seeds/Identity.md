## What is identity?
What does it mean to have a self (in the information age)?
How might we create forms of identity that allow people to use it as a useful tool when it's needed and to cast it away when it's not?
### Identity is relational
Identity occurs at the point of interaction. Relational vs static
Who am I in relation to this song, book, person, thing? Not, who am I?

“ultimately, in the sovereign individual, you will always be articulated and defined in relation to the relationships you have with others, your relationship to the space that you're in, your relationship to institutions and extitutions. And there is a truth that's true to you, but that's always defined in your situatedness.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

Seeing like a state- names of streets change depending on which direction you are looking from.

‘As @chaosprime — a popular alt — once said, ‘Cognition is not a discrete process taking place inside your head. It isn’t even a discrete process taking place inside your body. It’s a web extending everywhere, with dense nodes pulling it this way and that, synchronizing and desynchronizing, making models of each other.’” ([Being Yourselves](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3128))

Identities coevolve through interactions with others. Henrik Karlsson
### Identity is about shared contexts & narratives
Identity is about the meaning you give to things in a context– shared context + meaning creates shared identities. “making sense of life’s intricate patterns through lenses of novel or deep-rooted assumptions.” [[the next era of third spaces as crucibles for meaning-making]]

Being a woman- everyone is different but there is a shared context which creates a shared identity.

Shared language breeds identity.

Communicating based on shared context: “one of the struggles of cross-cultural communication is finding common ground —a task that is considerably easier when speaking with people from your own cultural background, whereas ‘speaking with people from another culture it often takes longer to explain the context, background or meaning of a story than it does to actually tell the story.´” (Indigenous AI)

Low status to high status can vary situationally. Book smart vs street smart.

“A brand is a cultural phenomenon that emerges only when the product, the copywriting, the mission statement, the founder's vision, the designer's aesthetics, or the employees' actions come into contact with people… **a brand operates as a consensus system, facilitating a consistent set of beliefs across people.”** (**[Headless Brands](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/159476)**)

Who is allowed to question your identity and on what grounds? Being Venezuelan, being Portuguese, being from nowhere. What narratives do I have the credibility to participate in?

Identity often tied to job, employer, skillset. (Economic context)

“Self-made”, the self-made man
### The human and planetary bodies sit at the core of identity
The body is the original breeding ground for identity. Staying alive as an objective creates identity around the experience of having a body, caring for the body: food rituals, birth, sex & death, how to present the body, the roles each body can perform.

“interrogating the reality of the body. The embodied nature of it…” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727)) allows for a deeper connection with identity and a playfulness around being.

“Your actual body (of which the eyes and fingers and amygdala are subsystems) gets a grip on the world of cyberspace through the interface of rectangle and software, and presses up against the bodies of others through this medium... In person, we wear clothes rather than go around naked; I think it’s possible for the entire appearance of the body to be bracketed, as with clothing. _The internet is a type of garment.” (_**[Social Media Consciousness](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/4928177)**_)_

Identity is a function of context, place is a necessary context.
### The social and technological protocols of identity are commons.
The commons– identity as a commons: common practices, common protocols, the range of actions which are available, and how those actions can be performed is something we hold in common. The software or socialware of identity is a commons.

“Today the sphere of public opinion, including user review sites, personal blogs, and social media threads, constitute a new part of a brand. They are the "surface" of the brand's invisible body, against which official brand messaging can be compared and validated**.**” (**[Headless Brands](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/159476)**) What it does vs what it says it does, shared context
### Identity goes beyond ownership
“I think in kind of modernity, there's been an overconflation of property and identity. So if we conceive of kind of property as it's most often a kind of collection of different rights, so right to access, but also often the right to exclude others. And often those rights are attributed to kind of one agent, one identity, or one group or entity… And that's where a lot of the ideas of self sovereign identity, I think, ideologically come from.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

The individual as the agent that will overcome the state… institutions are evil, “the individual is the ultimate broker of property” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

If identities are relational, how can we own them? Identity is not a kind of property but a relational inventory that determines how you relate to the world.

How can our active conceptions of identity invoke agency “not restricted to our ability to invoke property rights”? ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

“There are two ideas at the core of decentralized identities. One is that subjects have private control over their identifying data and can grant, revoke, and share partial access to it. While not necessary for its implementation, a corollary to this idea is that subjects can use such an identity standard as a universal login across platforms.” (**[Inventories, Not Identities](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3134149)**)

“Often there's this conflation between the data that you produce and you own and your own identity.” Just because you can move your data around that doesn’t necessarily make your identity more “free”. This model ignores not just the idea of identity as relational, but also the infrastructure needed to support identity ie. food, water, place. ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

Neither self-sovereign nor institutionalized, but in relationship. “ownership is less about financial upside and more about social influence.” (**[Reputation in Web3](https://web.getmatter.com/)**)

### Identity is about collecting reputation
Reputation vs identity, reputation is a function of identity, it shows how you have related to things in the past, which gives some measure as to how you will relate to things in the future. Building reputation opens up more doors for what you are allowed to do.

“your bank account is your stored wealth, but your real name is your stored reputation.” (**[The Pseudonymous Economy](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/19210#highlight-2371253)**)

Keeping bad actors or saboteurs from depleting resources, both physical and emotional, is one of the most important parts of governance: bad reputation.

If people can erase their identities and start again, you can’t stop people from repeatedly cheating the system, building reputation and then cashing out when the benefit is high.

To what extent do we need to attach a persistent bad reputation to known criminals?

How do we implement context specific accountability in media, which merge the economic with the technical propagation of messages?

### Identity is what you are allowed to do.
“Who is allowed to participate? On what grounds are they allowed to participate? What are the rules by which we construct participation based on who people are? What kinds of people we want involved? Who's not at the table? All of these questions come back to identity, and yet identity then relates to all of these questions.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

If you think you are the kind of person you will do something, you will do something ie. “I am the kind of person who cares about animals” vs “Eating meat is manly”

What’s required, in a democratic society, to prove that you have enough stake, residence, citizenship in a place in order for your vote to count or for you to have a vote? What constitutes enough of a stake or residence in a community in order to have a say beyond just the holding of a governance token? ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

“So I think there's one kind of technical frame that could see identity as basically the ability to act, or like in your piece, the ability to sign. So identity becomes kind of basically performing an action rather than a kind of static identity, which is identified within a bureaucratic framework and then operationalized from there.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

“The important thing from a logging-into-software perspective is not _who you are_, but _what you are authorized to do_. So, **Keys, not IDs**.” (**[Soulbinding Like a State](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/9365926#highlight-2372689)**)

### Your network defines your identity
You are likely to do something if it is normal to do that thing in the environment you are in. You become the average of the 7 people you hang out with most. “the shape of the network that we participate in informs the kind of relationships we can have, and therefore the kind of identity that we are capable of performing.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

Webs of trust, I vouch for this person , I confirm this.

CirclesUBI: you can signal that you trust someone enough in that context, creating a web of trust.

How to avoid P2P identity becoming a popularity contest? Lots of people trust Dr Oz, but that doesn’t mean he’s a good doctor.

Highly regulated environments like KYC, medicine, institutions are needed. What institutions have the right to say you are a person, you are trustworthy, you are a doctor, you are a citizen? What about the identity and reputation of that institution itself?

### Legibility allows for control & profit from identity
Creating static, “legible” identities is a form of control and simplification. Static indentities turn us into a caricature of ourselves, preventing development and growth. “Criticizing those who confront you with perspectives that are hard to integrate into your performance. Shutting down authentic relating in favor of crude brand management.” ([Being Yourselves](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3128))

“When we say “on-chain identity”, we are gesturing toward something that is standardized, certified, registered, recorded. An API for quantifying people. This is reflected in the use-cases cited for on-chain identity, such as one-person-one-vote polling, credit scores, credentials, reputation, mutual rating, resumes, background checks.” (**[Soulbinding Like a State](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/9365926#highlight-2372689)**)

At the same time as decentralized Headless Brands are being born, letting go of the illusion of control, we are expected ourselves to be tightly controlled brands with singular narratives. Decentralization, de-individualization, as rejection of legibility.

What do we want to make our identities legible for vs not legible? What parts?

- To have agency
    - For governance purposes, to do work & participate
    - To connect with people and experiences that can help us further develop our identity. “Twitter is a type of echolocation — you learn about who you’re becoming from the followers and replies that bounce back to you… The algorithm seems to route tweets to the very people who will understand what the hell you’re talking about.” ([Being Yourselves](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3128)) Machine Love
- Not for exploitation, state, institutional, advertisers shaping what we consume and experience

It’s possible to identify someone by analyzing their network and transactions, which is why it’s important for identities to be context specific and not global: avoid legibility and breaches of privacy. (**[Inventories, Not Identities](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3134149)**)

### Identity is like a mask or clothing you can change into and out of
Clothing affects identity, african ritual masks. “Clothing can be an external expression of the role or the identity that you're wearing at any given moment.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727))

Spoken protocols can be a form of mask that creates identity, linguistic costumes.

How do designed spaces affect identity? It changes what you are allowed to do there, how you can express yourself. The structure of a space is also a kind of mask.

“designing technical or sociotechnical systems that conceive of identity as a form of clothing rather than as a form of being.” ([KB7 Fireside](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/43356727)) You’ll happily let friends borrow a sweater, you’ll inherit a sweater from your grandfather.

### A pseudonym is a kind of mask that can represent a different identity
Pseudonym is wearing a mask, masks can help explore ideas and emotions to raw to touch with real identity, dangerous. “Digital masks broaden your range of expression beyond what’s possible with your physical body. With a mask, you can reveal parts of yourself that would otherwise remain hidden on the inside.” ([Being Yourselves](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3128))

Connection between alt selves and publishing “in progress” work via digital gardens- no pressure to have a coherent voice

Rather than an escape from self, alt identities “teach you that your legal identity is also a kind of mask — an ever-evolving ‘montage of loosely assembled parts’.” (**[Inventories, Not Identities](https://web.getmatter.com/entry/3134149)**)

Pseudonyms shield you from retaliation but not accountability: your reputation is held accountable, not every part of your life.

Pseudonyms might reduce discrimination against certain identity groups ie. in hiring situation.

Should we have different identities for speaking, earning and legalities?

Google multiple account support is a form of pseudonymous identity.
