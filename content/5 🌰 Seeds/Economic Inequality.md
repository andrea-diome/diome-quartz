It’s not just platforms with an advertising business model that are driving polarisation. Extremism grows from the fears and frustrations of economic inequality. Ever since the rise of neoliberalism in the 1980s, our core institutions have been dismantled and privatised, while checks and balances for companies have been all but erased. The dismantling of our institutions also means that algorithms are being used to systematically discriminate against those who need support. All of this has led to the highest levels of inequality since feudal times.
