## What is it?
Moral ecosystems refer to networks of individuals and communities that are united by shared moral beliefs, values, and practices. Moral ecosystems are thriving on the diversity and dynamism of digital and social media platforms, which allow for a broad and fluid exchange of ideas, narratives, and values across global and local scales.
