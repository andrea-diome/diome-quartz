In the mid 20th century, economists widely believed that economics should focus on commercial markets, whereas most of the other things that mattered in life should be understood morally through societal values.

However, this didn’t fit the the reductionist view of the world, which wanted to search for ‘objective’ answers. Morality and values were too subjective and therefore not ‘real’ or scientific. Neoliberalism proposed that in fact the market was a way to create knowledge, because it quantified all of these subjective experiences and exposed them numerically through prices.

Starting with Ronald Regan in the US and Margaret Thatcher in the UK, governments deregulated market and removed all protections so that markets could regulate themselves. They also went to town privatizing every part of society because the market new best. This campaign has led to the widest inequality since feudal times and the growth of global monopolies that are bigger than many countries.
