Infrastructure is our communal body - the shared systems through which we move, breathe, communicate, and interact with the world around us. Just as our individual bodies are the vessels through which we experience and influence our environment, our societal infrastructure is the collective body through which we manifest our shared values, social structures, and relationship with the natural world.

In this section, we'll examine various aspects of the infrastructure:
