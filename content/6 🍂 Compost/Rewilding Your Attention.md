"Back in the early 00s, the dominance of Windows made a rich target for viruses and worms. These days, mobile app-developers face the problem of incredibly un-diverse app stores — you’ve got Apple’s App Store and Google Play, and that’s mostly it. It’s a landscape with precisely two crops.
