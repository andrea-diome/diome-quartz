## Highlights
"A good public digital space welcomes everyone, helps us understand the world, connect across divides, and act together."

#### Opportunities & Insights
"'- Digital social platforms are being used as public spaces - User-friendly helps users accomplish goals easily, efficiently, and elegantly. Public-friendly helps the public gather and achieve goals easily, efficiently, and elegantly. - When creating public spaces, no single algorithm or design will work for everyone.'"

#### Regenerative Digital Spaces
![[civic-signals-process.png]]
"What if we look at digital life through the lens of physical space? Humans have designed spaces for public life for millennia" 
"Develop programming – social activities – that draw different groups in, without over-optimizing for any one group" 
"Are designed to be physically accessible and attractive to many different populations" 
"Engage stewards, leaders, and maintainers who can do the labor of community-building" 
"Public spaces – places like parks, libraries, and town squares – are an essential part of functioning pluralistic societies." 
"Offer visual cues as to what kinds of behavior are invited in the space" 
"Public life has moved into electronic media (radio, TV, cable, websites), and digital platforms like Facebook, Twitter, or TikTok. These forums act like public spaces, but they weren’t built to prioritize public goals." 
"Are designed in partnership with the communities that use them."
