## Highlights
"We must meet our share sustainability goals if we hope to avoid the devastation of irreversible ecological collapse, massive global inequality and climate-driven social and political unrest"

"The potential for sweeping environmental regulation could soon make sustainability standards compliance the baseline."
"Regenerative transformation will define how we live & work in the next 10 years. Products marketed as sustainable accounted for just 16% of the consumer goods market in 2019 but delivered half of all market growth."
"From 2013 to 2019, sustainable brands outpaced the growth of conventionally marketed products by more than 500%"

"Today’s companies must serve business shareholders and societal stakeholders in equal measure, leading both individual organizations and entire communities into a more sustainable future" 
"Sustainability is a necessary step on the road to reversing the damage we’ve already caused and regenerating both people and the planet" 
"As firms make regenerative design their default approach, they will not only uncover opportunities to realize better products and services—they will also create more dynamic, sustainable and adaptive systems for their products and services to live within." 
"Because disruption and sustainability both require profound paradigm shifts, sustainable innovation offers one of the most promising opportunity spaces for newcomers and incumbents alike." 
"Organizations that fully understand the business value of sustainability will recognize that this strategic transition is an opportunity for innovation that will provide quick movers a competitive advantage" 
"By demonstrating commitment to the same social, economic and environmental values as the customers and employees they want to attract, organizations can advance their business goals while also deepening their sustainability impact." 
"By integrating sustainability and equity as core values, organizations can transform their own businesses while pressuring competitors to follow suit, helping to build a thriving community that’s positioned to take on some of the biggest challenges facing our world." 
"By seeking and prioritizing partnerships with other sustainably minded organizations, firms can incentivize the adoption of sustainable practices across their business ecosystem and accelerate progress toward global climate goals" 
"disruption means acting as a powerful, transformative force for change" 
". Business leaders should also seek inspiration from concepts and values common to indigenous traditions across the world— equilibrium, harmony, reciprocity, diversity and accountability." 
"If digital transformation defined how we live and work in the last ten years, regenerative transformation will define the next 10." 
"Relevant KPIs: Increased revenue from new sustainable product and service lines, Improved opportunities to reduce operational and supply chain impact,  Long-term strategic planning that looks beyond quarterly results, Transformed company culture and mission set around a regenerative vision, Increased engagement with customers, employees and local, global communities, Improved brand reputation and increased brand awareness" 

"Sustainable Experiences: Shaping experiences, products, environments and services that reflect an urgent, comprehensive understanding of the social, environmental and climate facets of sustainability." 
"Next Economy Brands: Redefining strategic purposes and business models to create alignment with the social values, consumer expectations and environmental regulations of the Next Economy." 
"Regenerative Ventures: Co-creating the next generation of sustainable change-making organizations through Corporate Venture Building and Startup Growth" 
"Next Economy Teams: Designing organizational tools, structures and strategies that strengthen internal alignment and efficiency while fostering sustainable innovation and growth."
