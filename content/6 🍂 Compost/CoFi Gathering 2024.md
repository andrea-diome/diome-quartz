In Personam vs In Rem: once you lock a relationship into an object you can't get it out again.
1. In Personam:
- Latin for "against the person"
- Refers to a court's jurisdiction over a specific individual or entity
- The court has the power to make decisions that are binding on the person
- Typically used in cases involving personal rights and obligations
- Example: A lawsuit against a specific person for breach of contract
2. In Rem:
- Latin for "against the thing"
- Refers to a court's jurisdiction over property rather than a person
- The court has the power to determine rights in, or ownership of, a piece of property
- Often used in cases involving real estate, maritime law, or asset forfeiture
- Example: A foreclosure proceeding against a specific piece of real estate
