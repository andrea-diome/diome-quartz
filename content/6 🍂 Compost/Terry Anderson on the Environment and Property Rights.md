* When we think about what environmental problems are to most people, they don't think of it this way, but they're really property rights problems. You are dumping your gunk into my receptacle, be it the air, the water, my land. And I want some recourse. And if we have a well-functioning legal system that respects and defends property rights, your dumping gunk into my receptacle means you have to pay me or cease and desist. And that's been the cornerstone of free market environmentalism.
