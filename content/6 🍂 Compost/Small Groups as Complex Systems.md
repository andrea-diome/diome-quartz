Although it is often not possible to identify the starting point of a new group with precision, we propose that a group "begins" when people who think of themselves as belonging to a new group interact with other members and start coordinating their actions for a collective purpose. Thus, group formation is both a cognitive and behavioral process. All groups form in some context, in which people and resources are available, to serve one or more purposes via collective action.
