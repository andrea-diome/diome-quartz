## Highlights
* In the United States, corporations capture the state. In China, the state rules corporations.
* The truth is that both Big Tech and China operate as monopolies — it’s just that the former exists in the private sector aiming at private gain, whereas the latter operates under the guise of a state clearly focused on maximizing collective national advantage.
* In a world in which we have to compete with state-run giants in China, which think on 50-year time horizons, quarterly capitalism simply doesn’t cut it anymore (not that it ever really did).
* while antitrust action is desperately needed to ensure a level playing field in the age of platform monopolies, we need to stop looking for a silver-bullet solution on competition.
