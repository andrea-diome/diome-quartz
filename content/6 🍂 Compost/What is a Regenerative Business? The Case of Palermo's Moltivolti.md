#### Moltivolti
![[moltivolti-model.png]]
"Moltivolti is both a for-profit and a non-profit organization, a hybrid business model we can categorize as a regenerative business."
"It’s purpose is to create a community-based dining experience, a hub for social integration and employment which serves the needs of the community beyond food and nourishment."
"Moltivolti is both a restaurant, and a co-working and meeting space."
"Moltivolti is also a co-working and meeting space for its community partners – a range of non-profit organizations which are part of the neighborhood and create value for community residents"
"The restaurant employs immigrants  and delivers an eclectic mix of Sicilian and world cuisine to the visitors. The customers of the restaurant are people from the neighborhood, tour operators and tourists, school children, and immigrants. The design of the space creates a multicultural dimensionality"
"Moltivolti is also a part of Addiopizzo, an anti-Mafia movement of local businesses."
