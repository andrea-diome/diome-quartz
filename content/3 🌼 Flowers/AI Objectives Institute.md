## What
The [AI Objectives Institute](https://ai.objectives.institute/) (AOI) is a collaborative research organization dedicated to exploring the intersections between AI alignment, economic regulation, institutional design and personal autonomy, aiming to mitigate risks associated with superhuman optimizing entities like corporations, political parties, and AI systems.
### Who
Founded by a collaborative group of researchers from leading AI industry and academic settings.
### Where & When
Launched in February 2023.
### Why
AOI recognizes the transformative potential of AI and the urgent need to align its objectives and behaviors with human-centric values and societal well-being. By drawing lessons from the successes and failures of aligning superhuman entities and applying advancements in AI safety and ethics, AOI seeks to guide AI towards supporting human prosperity and autonomy.
### How
- **Research and Development**: AOI undertakes both theoretical and practical research to create AI systems aligned with societal goals.
- **Collaborative Projects**: The institute brings together a diverse group of experts to innovate in AI alignment, institutional design, and enhancing personal autonomy.
- **Policy Advocacy and Education**: AOI raises awareness about AI alignment's significance for civilization's future and advocates for supportive policies and practices.
