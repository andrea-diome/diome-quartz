## What
[Additive Catchments](https://additivecatchments.com/) is an environmental technology firm offering real-time monitoring and management solutions to support sustainable practices and informed decision-making across sectors. Their flagship product, CMAAS® (Catchment Monitoring as a Service), provides accurate, up-to-date environmental data to government entities, agricultural sectors, insurance companies, infrastructure planners, and food supply chains.
## Who
The company collaborates with organizations like Thames Water, Siemens and Atkins Global.
## Where & When
Operates in the UK, pilot in development.
## Why
Additive Catchments aims to enhance environmental stewardship, promote transparency, and rebuild public trust, particularly in water companies. 
## How
### Key Principles
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Utilizing IoT technology for continuous environmental data collection.
- **Transparent Reporting**: Providing accessible, accurate data to demonstrate environmental impacts and regulatory compliance.
- **Community Engagement**: Encouraging public participation and trust through Community-Based Participatory Research (CBPR).
- **Flood Risk Management**: Offering data for managing flood risks and coastal erosion.
- **Support for Agriculture**: Helping farmers manage environmental impacts and improve sustainable practices.
### Functions
The customer application includes:
- **River Health Map**: A user-friendly interface for monitoring environmental health and submitting observations.
- **Personal Environmental Dashboard**: Customizable tracking and alerts for environmental conditions.
- **Citizen Science**: A portal for community-contributed environmental data, fostering active public participation.
- **Learning Section**: Educational resources and collaborations for different age groups to promote environmental awareness.
- **Public Forum and Social Hub**: A space for discussions, feedback, and engagement with local environmental groups.
- **Virtual Events and Workshops**: Online events fostering environmental knowledge and community involvement.
- **Real-time Feedback and Reporting System**: A mechanism for immediate user feedback and transparent acknowledgment.
- **Custom Alerts and Notifications**: Personalized system to keep users informed about environmental and community activities.
### Business Models
- **Subscription Model**: Offering data monitoring services to water companies on a 'data-to-the-desk' basis.
- **Consultancy for LPAs & Developers**: Assisting with nutrient neutrality decisions, enabling housing project development.
