## What
The [Whole Earth Codec](https://codec.earth/) is an innovative autoregressive, multimodal foundation model that redefines the concept of observatories by focusing inward on Earth itself. This project shifts from the traditional cosmos-oriented observatory to a distributed planetary observatory that uses ecological and environmental data. By integrating various streams of data from across the biosphere into a singular, comprehensive model, the Whole Earth Codec aims to enable the planet to observe and understand itself on an unprecedented scale.

## Why
The motivation stems from the necessity to broaden the observational scope of AI models to encompass non-human, ecological data, to unlock a form of planetary intelligence or sapience. By doing so, the project anticipates fostering a deeper, more nuanced understanding of Earth's systems and their interconnections.
## How
- **Sensing Layer**: Utilizing a global network of sensors to gather diverse ecological data, from bioacoustic signals to atmospheric conditions, creating a comprehensive dataset that spans the non-human realms of the planet.
- **Cross-Modal Integration**: Developing an AI model capable of processing and synthesizing multimodal data into a unified representation of Earth, allowing for the detection of patterns and correlations across different types of ecological information.
- **Generative and Analytical Capabilities**: Beyond analysis, the model harbors the potential to generate predictive models and creative solutions for ecological management and conservation, from optimizing weather patterns to designing resistant crop genomes.
