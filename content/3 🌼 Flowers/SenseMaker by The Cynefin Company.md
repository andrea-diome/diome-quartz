## What
[SenseMaker®](https://thecynefin.co/get-sensemaker/) is a sophisticated web-based software tool designed to aid decision-makers in comprehensively understanding and addressing complex challenges. It utilizes the Cynefin Framework and principles of anthro-complexity to facilitate nuanced sense-making and informed decision-making within organizations and communities.
## Who
Developed by [The Cynefin Company](https://thecynefin.co/).
## Where & When
Based in the UK.
## Why
In a world characterized by ever-increasing complexity and interconnectedness, traditional linear approaches to problem-solving and decision-making often fall short. 
## How
### Key principles
- **Complexity and Context Sensitivity**: Emphasizes understanding the intricate dynamics within systems and tailoring responses to specific contexts.
- **Engagement and Co-creation**: Encourages active participation from various stakeholders, leveraging collective intelligence to uncover insights.
- **Adaptive Learning**: Promotes an iterative approach to decision-making, where strategies evolve in response to new information and changing circumstances.
### Functions
- **Frameworks and Engagements**: Users can create or utilize pre-designed frameworks to gather and analyze data relevant to specific challenges or goals.
- **Data Collection and Analysis**: Through web or app-based collectors, respondents contribute data which is then visualized and interpreted, highlighting key trends, patterns, and "adjacent possibles."
- **Actionable Insights**: SenseMaker not only aids in understanding complex scenarios but also guides the formulation of strategic interventions, enabling organizations to move towards desired outcomes effectively.
### Business Models
Organizations can access the platform through subscription models, purchasing pre-designed frameworks, or engaging with SenseMaker® consultants for tailored solutions.
