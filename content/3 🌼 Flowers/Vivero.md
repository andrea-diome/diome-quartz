## What
[Vivero](https://earthregenerators.org/vivero/) is a groundbreaking initiative designed to gather community resources towards the stewardship of Earth, acting as a participatory trust for regenerative practices. By leveraging the power of community-led dialogue, mutual aid, non-profit fiscal hosting, and advanced technological solutions, Vivero aims to curate and fund regenerative work, nurturing projects from their nascent stages to their implementation in the real world. 
## Who
The project is led by the [Earth Regenerators network](https://earthregenerators.org/), Possible Planet, and Serve Commons PBC.
## Where & When
Launched on March 23rd, 2023, Vivero operates as a web and mobile application.
## Why
Vivero aims to address the urgent need for a dedicated commons accounting mechanism that allows the Earth Regenerators network to organize and direct resources effectively towards community-sourced initiatives for Earth stewardship. Vivero represents a critical step towards cultivating a regenerative gift economy, facilitating mutual aid and care within and across communities.
## How
- **Regenerative Finance Platform**: Vivero acts as a treasury combining an endowment and giving balance, pooling financial resources for community-directed support of regenerative projects.
- **Web3 Integration**: Utilizing blockchain technology to grow a common pool of resources, Vivero leverages community staking positions to generate returns for grantmaking and community treasury, mirroring the dynamics of an endowment fund.
- **Participatory Treasury**: Through the Vivero app, participants can nominate, discuss, and direct contributions to various regenerative initiatives, including individual fellowships, grassroots projects, and social enterprises.
