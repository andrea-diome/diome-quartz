## What
[The JCR Club](https://www.thejcrclub.com/) is a collection of 1,000 unique digital collectibles (NFTs), each linked to an acre of Jalama Canyon Ranch. It represents membership in a community dedicated to supporting regenerative agriculture and offers members exclusive access to ranch products.
### Who
[White Buffalo Land Trust](https://www.whitebuffalolandtrust.org/monitoring-research), Sandhi Wines.
### Where & When
Jalama Canyon Ranch, present time.
### Why
To raise funds and engage a community in supporting the regeneration of Jalama Canyon Ranch while offering exclusive benefits from the ranch's produce, fostering a sustainable and regenerative agriculture movement.
### How
#### Key Principles
- Supports and funds regenerative agriculture.
- Connects members with the land and its yield.
- Offers tangible benefits through membership.
- Promotes sustainable and ecological farming practices.
#### Functions
- Provides members with guaranteed allocations of ranch produce like wine and olive oil.
- Hosts member-exclusive perks, events and experiences.
- Engages members in volunteer opportunities for hands-on involvement in regenerative practices.
- Depending on the collectible’s ecological site, members receive specific data, artwork, and related benefits.
#### Business Models
Membership fees, sales of ranch products, and experiences provide funding for regenerative practices at Jalama Canyon Ranch. Digital collectibles serve as both a membership pass and an innovative funding mechanism.
