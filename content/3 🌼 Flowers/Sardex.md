## What
[Sardex]() is an innovative local currency system established in Sardinia, Italy, designed to stimulate the local economy, foster community ties, and contribute to sustainable development by providing an alternative to traditional monetary systems.
## Who
Initiated by five local entrepreneurs. Since its inception, Sardex has gained significant traction, with over 4,000 businesses participating and generating a turnover of approximately €140 million.
## Where & When
Originating in Sardinia in 2008. 
## Why
The creation of Sardex was driven by the desire to mitigate the adverse effects of the global financial crisis on Sardinia by establishing a self-sustaining economic system that encourages local trade and investment, reduces dependence on traditional credit sources, and supports community-based economic development.
## How
Members can transact in the digital currency Sardex, receiving credits or incurring debits in their accounts, promoting a fluid exchange of goods and services without the reliance on external financial systems.
