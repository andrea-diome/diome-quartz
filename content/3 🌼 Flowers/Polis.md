## What
[Polis](https://pol.is/) is an innovative open-source platform designed for survey research that harnesses the power of data science. It functions as a real-time tool for gathering, analyzing, and understanding the collective opinions of large groups, expressed in their own words, supported by advanced statistical methods and machine learning algorithms.
## Who

## Where & When
Has been used in the US & Taiwan.
## Why
Polis was developed to address the challenges of traditional survey methods that often fail to capture the dynamic nature of public opinion and complex societal issues. 
## How
### Key Principles
- **Participant-Driven Surveys**: Polis shifts the creation of survey dimensions to the participants, allowing the survey to evolve with participation.
- **Adaptive Engagement**: The platform adapts to incoming responses, ensuring efficient use of participants' time by displaying comments semi-randomly for evaluation.
- **Open Source Software**: Polis is freely available under the AGPL3 license, encouraging community-based development and customization.
### Functions
- **Statement Submission**: Participants submit concise text statements on a given topic.
- **Voting System**: Other participants vote on these statements, indicating agreement, disagreement, or neutrality.
- **Cluster Analysis**: An algorithm sorts participants into groups based on similar responses, facilitating the identification of consensus statements.
- **Interactive Overview**: A live, evolving graphical representation of the conversation's ecosystem is available to participants for review and further engagement.
