It's not enough to capture carbon or even regenerate our ecosystems. The problem we are facing is primarily a failure in coordination, therefore we need a complete overhaul of our social ecosystems.

We're now facing critical tipping points in the earth's biome, but what are the social tipping points which we need to address in order to change our relationship with the earth?

Theories about how societies collapse due to increased complexity and decreasing ability to solve problems. The real problem is in our information/social technologies (cultural, political, economic structures)

Rather than linear, growth-consumed systems, we need systems that are:

> **Biophillic:** Create the conditions for life to flourish, love of life. Love = working towards the things you value.

> **Biomimetic:** Reflect the complex patterns of life. Evolution, circularity, continuous flows.

[[Ecocultures]]
Ecosystems shape and are shaped by material technologies, but also by information technologies. Informational tech is human's ecological niche, it's what allows us to adapt to our environment. 

In healthy systems, careful observations and understandings of nature shape cultures over time, and in turn these cultural narratives and institutions [guide actions towards nature](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/environmental-conservation/article/interdisciplinary-progress-in-approaches-to-address-socialecological-and-ecocultural-systems/7D74F7B3F1E1E4B3820B9592B1A03100). In the broken system in which we live, our cultural narratives guide us to destroy ecosystems in the pursuit of abstracted wealth. To change our actions towards our ecosystems, our information technologies need to be redefined.

[[Cultural Colonialism]]
[Thiongo wrote in Decolonising the Mind:](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/986506/7/Indigenous_Protocol_and_AI_2020.pdf)
> *The most important domination was the mental universe of the colonized, the control, through culture, of how people perceived themselves and their relationship to the world. Economic and political control can never be complete or effective without mental control. To control a people’s culture is to control their tools of self-definition in relationship to others.

Lets start with social media platforms...

There has been a huge controversy recently about how TikTok and other social media platforms are shaping children's brains. The truth is that we always program children's brains, that's what makes humans unique. We are born as blank slates to be programed into humans through our information technologies. The problem is not that we are programing our children but *how* we are programing them, *for what,* and *who gets to decide.*

[[Social Media Moulds Identities & Relationships]]
Information technologies are not just a tool, but a [transformer of individual consciousness](https://www.jstor.org/stable/43501355). “the process of socialization in a person forms a style of thinking, an individual stock of knowledge that determines our behavior, interests and values.” ([Source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356407621_Mass_consciousness_and_mass_culture_under_the_influence_of_contemporary_challenges))

The problem with social media is that we are programing children to have short attention spans, to generate and interact with content that appeals to those attention spans, and above all to adopt behaviors that generate profit for the platform. Social media platforms train children to be consumers.

In a recent study, children were shown the silhouettes of company logos and commonly found leaves. Most were able to identify the logos but not the leaves.

On the other side, education systems take away our agency and shape us into production machines for the market.

We are unanchored from community, no longer people of place but people of markets. People of markets are able to better succeed in the world because they can easily identify and respond to the needs of the market. To regenerate ecocultures, we need to become people of place.
