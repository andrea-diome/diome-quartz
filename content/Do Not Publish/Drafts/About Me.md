## draft 1
Hello and welcome! I am a technology researcher, designer, and digital anthropologist. My lifelong love for technology, stemming from childhood days glued to screens, evolved into a fervent desire to leverage it to create a better world. However, a personal brush with a debilitating injury made me realize the imbalance we've created – not just in our individual lives but in our collective relationship with the planet.

For years, I operated under the digital spell, embracing the culture of startups and relentless innovation. But my own physical breakdown, mirroring that of our precious Earth, led me on a journey to question the very essence of technology's role in our lives. Can it truly bring about the societal equilibrium we so desperately need? Or are we simply amplifying disparities and ecological degradation?

Diome, my venture, embodies this exploration. Through it, I aim to promote an understanding of regenerative principles and apply them to digital technology. Borrowing from indigenous wisdom and the organic rhythms of nature, my mission is to design technologies that are not extractive but nurturing, not divisive but unifying.

In this digital realm, I don't just wish to change the way we interact with technology, but also shift our perspective from an "I-It" to an "I-Thou" relationship, acknowledging the inherent interconnectedness of all life. By crafting an ecology of technology, we can bridge the virtual and the real, weaving a fabric of existence where both can thrive.

Join me as we reimagine our relationship with technology, grounding it not in commercial profit but in the flourishing of all life on our planet. Let's co-create a regenerative future where technology serves as the harmonious bridge between humanity and the vibrant web of life around us. Welcome to a journey of connection, respect, and regeneration.

## draft 2
Hello! I am a digital anthropologist, technology researcher, and passionate advocate for a regenerative future. With a rich background that spans everything from digital entrepreneurship to design innovation, I am deeply attuned to the intersections of technology, society, and individual well-being. Through my work, I strive to reimagine the digital landscape in service of a harmonious and resilient civilization.

**1. From Digital Dreamer to Realistic Visionary** Early in my career, I was entranced by the luminous promise of technology. Aspiring to be a digital entrepreneur, I believed that the right code, platform, or innovation could reshape our world for the better. But as the years progressed, I recognized a glaring disconnect. Technology's promise was often overshadowed by the consequences of its unchecked growth and the narrower interests it served.

**2. Personal Revelations from Physical Struggles** My life took an unexpected turn with a debilitating back injury, an ailment arising from endless hours in the digital realm. This personal setback wasn't just physical—it was profoundly reflective. It made me question our society's collective posture, not just in how we sit or stand, but in how we align ourselves with the world around us.

**3. The Paradox of the Digital Age** Technology has woven a fascinating web around us. It brings people closer, yet often distances them from their immediate surroundings. As an observer of human behavior, I've seen how our increasing digital entwinement can lead to feelings of disconnection, both from our environment and our own inner selves.

**4. Re-envisioning Value and Purpose** Our current systems place undue emphasis on economic gains, often overlooking broader values like ecological health, community well-being, and personal fulfillment. It's time to redefine success, to move beyond mere financial metrics, and to appreciate the less tangible but equally essential aspects of our existence.

**5. Embracing an I-Thou Perspective** Philosopher Martin Buber's I-Thou concept has deeply resonated with me. It's about viewing ourselves, other beings, and even technology as entities deserving of mutual respect and understanding, rather than mere tools or objects. By applying this philosophy, I believe we can foster technology that nurtures rather than numbs.

**6. Towards a Regenerative Digital Ecosystem** I've embarked on an exciting journey to champion a regenerative digital future. In this vision, technology isn't about relentless extraction but about restoration, care, and sustainable growth. Digital platforms and solutions should serve as extensions of our collective consciousness, amplifying our ability to heal, connect, and thrive.

Join me as we navigate this evolving digital tapestry, seeking paths that reinforce our shared humanity and our planet's boundless potential.
