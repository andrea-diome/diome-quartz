**3. Deciphering Value**

**3.1. Subjective Nature**: Value is inherently subjective, varying between individuals based on personal preferences, needs, and societal influences.

**3.2. Intrinsic vs. Perceived Value**: While certain items like gold might have intrinsic value due to their rarity or usefulness, other items or services derive value from societal perceptions or artificial scarcity.
