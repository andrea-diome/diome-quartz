The relationship between data, information, knowledge, and wisdom is often represented as a hierarchy or continuum, commonly known as the DIKW pyramid, renamed here to the Noetic Pyramid for increased fanciness. The 4 core elements are:
1. Data: Raw facts and figures without context or interpretation.
2. Information: Data that has been processed, organized, or given context.
3. Knowledge: Information that has been internalized, understood, and can be applied.
4. Wisdom: The ability to apply knowledge effectively, with insight and good judgment.
