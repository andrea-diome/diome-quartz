To enable [[Self-Governance of The Commons]], a strong social contract must be in place that guides the usage and management of the resource.

>*[Incorporating the wisdom](https://www.wwf.org.au/ArticleDocuments/789/RegenerativeEconomyReport_Final280720.pdf.aspx?OverrideExpiry=Y) and guidance from Indigenous communities will reshape social contracts.*

> *In current anthropocentric social contracts, natural resources are viewed to be used exclusively by humans, to serve the needs of humanity and the needs of our current economic systems with a singular focus on economic growth.* [Source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/350491544_Towards_a_Natural_Social_Contract)
