> _For most of human history people have been guided by values. Moral notions of right and wrong. What’s beautiful and what’s not. What’s just and what’s unjust. In recent years we’ve been ruled by value. Numerical expressions of what’s valuable, what’s not, and to what degree. It’s turned out that Value is a lot more convenient than Values._ [Yancey Strickler](https://ideaspace.ystrickler.com/p/roundtable-what-gets-measured-and)
