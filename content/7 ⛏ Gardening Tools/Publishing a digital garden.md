## Obsidian as CMS
After comparing options such as Notion and Roam Research, I decided to go with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) as the backend due to its approach to longevity, flexibility, and visualization, as well as its community energy and expected future trajectory.
[[Using Obsidian]]

## With No Code Tools
In the interest of getting my work out there as soon as possible, I will likely start out with a no-code option and shift to Wordpress in the near future.

#tools 
- Obsidian Publish seems to be the easiest option, but doesn't not currently support Canvas
- [Flowershow](https://flowershow.app/docs/publish-tutorial) looks great and easy to use. 
- [BrainPress](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/brainpress-self-publish-your-obsidian-vault-supports-canvas/51104) is an open source publisher for Obsidian Vault that supports Canvas
- [Obsidian Minimal Theme by Kepano](https://github.com/kepano/obsidian-minimal)
- [How to embed a newsletter sign-up form](https://notes.joschua.io/qE8YopfwsaR3bXqsdhmB4J)
